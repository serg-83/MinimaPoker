Actions

  • Fold — Discard hand, forfeit pot
  • Check — Pass, no bet required
  • Call — Match current bet
  • Raise — Increase current bet

Payment Channel

All bets happen off-chain via an eltoo payment channel. Funds are locked on-chain and settled when the channel closes. Use Cooperative close for instant settlement, or Dispute to force on-chain resolution (~30 blocks).
